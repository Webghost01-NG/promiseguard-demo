PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, clearly handled.

Effective September 9, 2026

What PromiseGuard collects

When you sign in, PromiseGuard receives the account identifier and basic profile details supplied by Google, GitHub, or Slack, or stores the username and password verifier you create directly. It also stores the integration credentials you choose to add, the records you select for analysis, and the resulting run history.

How the data is used

PromiseGuard uses this information to authenticate you, isolate your workspace, retrieve only the GitHub, Notion, and Slack records you select, assess that evidence with Gemini, and apply external changes only after your explicit review and approval.

Service providers

The service runs on Render. Depending on the features you choose, data is also processed by Google, GitHub, Notion, and Slack under their respective policies. PromiseGuard does not sell personal information or use workspace evidence for advertising.

Storage and security

Integration credentials are encrypted on the server and are never returned to the browser after saving. Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies. Each account has a separate workspace. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

This hackathon deployment uses Turso-backed durable storage. Render may pause or restart the free web service, but stored accounts, encrypted connections, settings, sessions, and run history remain in the configured database.

Your choices

You can disconnect an integration by clearing its saved credential. You can stop using the service at any time. To request deletion of a test account or report a privacy concern, contact the project owner through the public source repository without posting credentials or other secrets.
